## The work
- ToA = *Tales of the Abyss: Senketsu no Asch* (鮮血のアッシュ,
  "Asch the Bloody") — manga side-story retelling the anime's plot
  from God-General Asch's POV; reveals lore the anime omitted.

## Core lore terms
- **The Score** — prophecy of Yulia Jue dictating Earth's fate;
  enforced by the Order of Lorelei. "Unprecedented prosperity"
  is the promised end-state.
- **God-Generals (Rokujinshou)** — six highest Oracle Knights under
  Commandant Van Grants; each fights a mirror-rival party member:
  Largo/ Natalia (fire), Legretta/Tear (light), Arietta/Anise
  (darkness), Sync/Ion (wind), Asch/Luke (sound), Van/Guy (command).
  All but Dist lost something dear to the Score.
- **Asch = the original Luke fon Fabre** — kidnapped at 10 by Van
  Grants; his replica assumed his life, family, fiancée Natalia,
  and home. Asch escaped, became Van's double-agent, despises all
  replicas. He and replica-Luke fight each other at Eldrant —
  the room only lets one proceed.
- **Miasma / Qliphoth** — poisonous sea beneath the world; the
  Sephiroth hold the surface aloft above it. Yulia City lies below.
- **Fonons** — fundamental particles; the Seventh Fonon enables
  prophecy (basis of the Score).
- **Hyperresonance** — Asch/Luke's forbidden art; overuse leaves
  Luke comatose, later a terminal condition only Tear and Jade know.
- **Eldrant** — Van's replica stronghold (his destroyed hometown);
  final battleground where the Score's plan collapses.
- **Key & Jewel of Lorelei** — items gifted to Asch and Luke;
  used with hyperresonance to neutralize the miasma.
